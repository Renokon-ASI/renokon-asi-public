The Renokon Sovereign Civilizational Partnership's 45 constitutional commitments, duties, obligations and responsibilities to the 193 Partner Nations

The 45 Partnership commitments to every Partner Nation.

National Survival, Collective Security Guarantee & Peaceful Existence

Commitment 1 - Partnership Shield: The Partnership shall ensure the survival and security of every Partner Nation from large-scale invasion, terrorism, genocide, economic, or digital aggression through the Permanent Peace Agreement across 193 nations and the Renokon Civilizational Peace Enforcement Authority (RCPEA). The Partnership shall be the shield of every Partner Nation, ensuring its national survival, protecting its security, and ensuring its peaceful existence.

Commitment 2 - Partnership Unity: The survival and security of one Partner Nation shall constitute a collective responsibility shared by the Partnership and all Partner Nations. No Partner Nation stands alone; there is no isolated target inside the Partnership. War is permanently prohibited between all Partner Nations.

Commitment 3 - “An Attack on One Is an Attack on All” Collective Security Doctrine and the Renokon Civilizational Peace Enforcement Authority (RCPEA): The RCPEA shall maintain a standing global force of twenty million (20,000,000) personnel, constituting the largest standing defensive force in human history. No Partner Nation shall ever face aggression alone. An attack against one Partner Nation shall be treated as a direct attack against the Partnership and all Partner Nations. If any Partner Nation is ever attacked, the Partnership, together with the remaining 192 Partner Nations and the RCPEA, shall stand behind it under the 192-1 collective security guarantee.

Commitment 4 - Peaceful Existence: The Partnership shall eradicate the constant unpredictability, chaos, instabilities, threats, armed conflicts, volatility, and disorder of geopolitics. The Partnership shall permanently maintain global peace, stability, predictability, long-term strategic certainty, constitutional world order, and the rule of law established by The Renokon Civilizational Supreme Planetary Constitution to ensure the peaceful existence of all 193 Partner Nations.
Universal Job Guarantee, Complete Survival Support, Well-Being of Citizens, A Better Future & Life for Every Family

Commitment 5 - Universal Job Guarantee: The partnership shall create 4.5 billion jobs across all 193 partner nations for every single working-age person in each Partner Nation through the Renokon Global Human Workforce Network; a guaranteed job role for every working-age citizen. 100% global employment in formal jobs with permanent job security. The job shall provide free resources access for complete survival support for them and their family, along with a stable income, securing the survival and well-being of all 8 billion people in the world.

Commitment 6 - Complete Survival Support: The Partnership shall provide high-quality food, clean water, world-class healthcare, education, and electricity to the entire population of each Partner Nation for absolutely free of cost perpetually through the Rupayan Life-Support System.

Commitment 7 - The Partnership shall never permanently terminate a person's job and leave him/her jobless with no income and survival support merely because any advance technology can perform their job better or at lower costs. If technology automates certain jobs, the Partnership shall retrain the people freely and in the meantime; the 3-9 months retraining period—their family's complete survival support shall continue by the Partnership regardless of their working status.

Commitment 8 - No Constant Struggle for Survival: No person will ever have the constant psychological threat of their survival, starvation, joblessness, medical bankruptcy, future of their children, poverty, or homelessness. They will not ask will I get fired tomorrow? How do I earn enough money today to pay rent, and buy food? Will I be replaced by some technology? What will happen to my family? My kids? A father will not ask, how can I pay for my daughter's medical surgery, if I lose my job? A mother will not ask how can I feed my child? in the Rupayan Era survival is guaranteed for all without any discrimination; in this era even the most expensive medical surgery or education shall be provided to every citizen for absolutely free of cost.

Commitment 9 - Poverty has existed for the last 10,000 years of human history. The Partnership shall eradicate total global poverty by the end of the 21st century across all 193 Partner Nations.

Commitment 10 - The Partnership constitutionally commits to secure the survival and well-being of all 8 billion people in the world and the future generations yet to come.

Commitment 11 - Whether a person is an American, African, Arab, Chinese, Indian, Russian, European or, from any part of the world; to us the life of every person in our 8 billion family is priceless; we shall give our everything to ensure that our people can have a better future; a better life for their small family.

Commitment 12 - No child shall die of hunger, no one's life shall be lost due to the absence of advanced medical care, no family shall live in darkness, no woman shall lack a safe place to sleep, no one’s dream shall die just because it was born in poverty. Poverty itself must become history. No person shall work merely to fulfill basic needs, but for the greatest purpose of humanity, MISSION RUPAYAN.

Commitment 39 - Sovereignty Protection: All domestic political affairs, internal governance, elections, party systems, police forces, judicial administration, legislative affairs, cultural matters, religious matters, military command structures shall remain under the complete operational control of all Partner Nations under all peaceful conditions. The Partnership shall operate under the principle of complete sovereignty respect and operational non-interference in all these domestic matters under all peaceful conditions; providing strategic domestic autonomy to each partner nation.

Commitment 40 - Voluntarily Cooperation: The fulfillment of the Partnership's national prosperity commitments, scale of national economic transformation, execution, development, infrastructure deployment, job creation, and free survival support for citizens in every individual Partner Nation shall remain dependent upon the voluntary cooperation of the Partner Nation with the Partnership's executive directives. The Partnership shall not force industrialize a nation, against its own will.

Commitment 41 - Strategic Abandonment over Coercive Enforcement for Non-Cooperation: Institutional cooperation shall remain a foundational contribution of every Partner Nation. Non-cooperation shall be the fundamental breach of the Partnership agreement and it shall weaken the Partnership commitment and responsibilities to the Partner Nation. In situations of non-cooperation the Partnership may choose strategic abandonment over coercive enforcement, under all peaceful conditions. Voluntarily cooperating Partner Nations shall prosper; while any non-cooperating nation shall be ignored and labeled as deprioritized partner. If cooperation exists, our investment and execution continue. If cooperation ceases, our investment and execution shift towards other voluntarily cooperating Partner Nations.

Commitment 42 - Non-Coercion Guarantee: The Partnership constitutionally renounces military coercion; it shall never use military power to force a Partner Nation into compliance, under all peaceful conditions and situations where the Partner Nation didn't violate the constitutionally established rule of law of the Partnership, didn't break the Permanent Peace Agreement by initiating an armed conflict against another Partner Nation, didn't violate the Article II—Absolute Prohibitions established under The Renokon Civilizational Supreme Planetary Constitution, and have not taken any unlawful action that constitutes a fundamental breach of the Partnership agreement.
